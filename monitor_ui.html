How to read this monitor

It warms up on 30 fresh observations (about 2½ minutes at the default interval), then compares each quote with up to 120 earlier observations. Median and median absolute deviation reduce the influence of individual spikes. Sustained price shifts trigger recalibration.

Alerts cover unusually wide spreads, large midpoint deviations, falling displayed bid or ask liquidity, and persistent imbalance. Stale quotes, failed requests, auctions, duplicate observations, and clock disagreement suppress current alerts.

Displayed size is only liquidity at the best bid or ask of the selected feed. Stock sizes remain in the provider’s reported round lots; no fixed shares-per-lot conversion is assumed. Polling misses events between requests, so recordings are not a full trade tape and cannot support the passive-fill backtest directly. The static backtesting report remains in docs/index.html.
